# Vision

> What Aionis is, why it exists, who it is for, and what it changes.

**What it is.** Aionis is a falsifiable, anti-leakage experiment harness for
cross-sectional equity prediction — not a cognitive system and not a trading bot.
Each phase poses one pre-registered, two-tailed question on monthly rank-IC of
S&P 500 PIT constituents and records the verdict, including null, honestly.

**Why it exists.** To make look-ahead bias and p-hacking *detectable* rather than
deniable. The `config_committed`-before-result ledger, point-in-time data, and
shared-fold differential turn "rerun-to-significance" into an auditable defect.

**Who it is for.** The research lead and future reviewers. Every claim is
traceable to a ledger row and a frozen config sha256.

**What it changes.** Whether a feature set adds *incremental* cross-sectional
predictability over a fundamentals-only baseline. The treatment-vs-baseline
rank-IC differential is the only falsifiable claim; null-with-tight-CI is the
intended publishable outcome.

**Explicit non-goals.**
- No learned generative world model — infeasible at MVP scale and leakage-prone.
- No black-swan prediction — only vulnerability, exposure, and scenario analysis.
- No CEO-personality diagnosis — only observable-behavior decision-style profiles.
