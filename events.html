Items are regex-extracted from the primary doc (nbsp/thin-space entities normalized) and mapped to ONE category by a rare-material-first precedence (delisting > merger completion > control change > non-reliance > officer changes > ... > exhibits); the full item list is preserved per row. Documents whose items cannot be extracted are counted as unclassified — never guessed. Filing-date point-in-time; 8-K/A amendments are new accessions (immutable).
