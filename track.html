方法 + 边界

Walk-forward calibration reliability (display-only, leakage-safe). For each realized OOS month T, the Platt calibration map is refit on all realized (score, forward-return) pairs STRICTLY before T, then T's tickers are predicted; T's own realized outcome scores the prediction (backward audit). Per-month OOS ECE shows whether the calibrated P(up) stays trustworthy as the realized sample grows; the pooled reliability diagram bins all walk-forward OOS predictions (mean predicted P vs empirical up-frequency). Only the 2-param display map is refit — the frozen LightGBM learner is NEVER touched, and no ledger / frozen surface / research estimator is affected. NOT a research claim and NOT a path to positive rank-IC (research verdict ledger #49: combined rank-IC −0.0088, NULL).
